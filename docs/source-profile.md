## License and terms (manual)

Reviewed 2026-10-02.

- **No explicit license.** Both ArcGIS items leave `licenseInfo` blank. The 311 item is tagged "Open Data" and lists `sacgis@cityofsacramento.org` as the contact. Its description says it "contains 311 calls that were last updated on or after January 1st, 2023"; the live minimum `DateUpdated` is 2024-01-01 (see Dates).
- **Governing terms:** the City of Sacramento [Open Data Terms of Use](https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/content/dam/portal/it/gis/open-data/OpenDataTermsOfUse.pdf), part of the city's [Open Data Policy](https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/content/dam/portal/it/gis/open-data/OpenDataPolicy.pdf). Main points:
  - Any user may access and use the data, including derivative works. Machine consumption counts as accepting the terms.
  - The data is "for informational purposes only" and provided **as is**, with no warranty of accuracy, completeness or timeliness. The city isn't liable for decisions made from it.
  - The user indemnifies the city for claims arising from their use of the data.
  - The city claims no IP in a dataset unless the page it's accessed from says so. Neither item page does.
  - The city may withdraw data or change the terms at any time. California law governs.
- **What this means for RiverCity Pulse:**
  - Using the data in a public portfolio demo is fine.
  - The README and the dashboard footer must say the data comes from the City of Sacramento 311 open data, links to the terms, is shown as is and may be incomplete, and that the project is not affiliated with or endorsed by the city.
  - Don't use the city seal or logo.
  - The dashboard must not present derived metrics (median days to close and so on) as official city figures.
  - The feed may disappear without notice. The raw layer (`raw.page`) and the reconcile guard already handle that.

Map decision (M0): neighborhood names match the boundary file exactly, so the dashboard uses the neighborhood choropleth. The council-district fallback isn't needed. `ref.neighborhood_alias` still guards against future renames.

Differences from the 2026-09-30 measurements in `plan.md`:
- Blank neighborhoods are real NULLs (about 351.5k), not `''`. About 321.6k of them are "Non City" rows. The remaining ~30k are city rows with no neighborhood, which get `NeighborhoodMissingInCity`.
- The 311 names don't have the inconsistent-slash problem relative to the polygons, which use the same spelling (for example `College/Glen`).
- CategoryLevel2 now has 303 values.
